firetable-grid

The table is the package's own <DataGrid>: column resizing is on because the component defaults it on, and drag-to-reorder is behind its reorderable prop, wired to the toggle below. No UI kit and no drag-and-drop library — the reorder runs on native HTML5 drag events and moveColumnWithinCategory() decides the result. The panel on the right is the engine's own state, live.
